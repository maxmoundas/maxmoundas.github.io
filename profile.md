## Work Experience

### AI Engineer — Vanderbilt University, Amplify Generative AI Innovation Center
**Duration:** May 2023 – Present

Lead engineer and researcher at Vanderbilt's Amplify Generative AI Innovation Center. Joined as one of 3 team members; the team has since grown to 7 full-time staff plus student developers (undergraduate and graduate). Manages student developers contributing to platform development and research.

**Amplify Platform:**
Amplify is an open-source enterprise generative AI platform purpose-built for higher education. What began as "can we give universities the ChatGPT experience without per-user subscription costs?" has grown into a community of tens of universities across the United States deploying and running Amplify. The platform provides full LLM access — AI assistants, agentic workflows, Office 365 integrations, file handling, API access to models — while keeping universities in control of their data and costs.

- Lead developer on Amplify alongside Jules White and a small team of engineers
- Platform serves 18,000+ users at Vanderbilt at an average cost of $3/user/month
- Deployed at and used by a growing community of universities nationwide
- Features built: memory systems, RAG Fusion, API integrations, artifact generation, admin monitoring tools, Office 365 integrations, agentic workflows
- More info: https://www.amplifygenai.org/ and https://www.cr8.io/

**Majk — Agentic Desktop Application:**
Majk is an agentic desktop application enabling university staff and researchers to complete real tasks with AI via prompting. Goes beyond chat: coordinates specialized AI agents in parallel to deliver finished work products. Comparable in capability to Claude Code but designed with an interface accessible to non-developers — helping university staff accomplish complex tasks and build AI applications and workflows.
- More info: https://www.cr8.io/

**Research & Publications:**
Authored and co-authored multiple peer-reviewed research papers on LLMs, prompt engineering, and code generation. Primary research paper has 15 citations. Upcoming paper covers a survey of guardrail and protection systems for AI — evaluating industry-standard guardrails against proposed protection systems, analyzing data shortcomings, and proposing methods to test AI system resilience against adversarial prompting.

**AI Training & Community:**

Completed 2 cohorts (Fall 2025 and Spring 2026), ~20 staff per cohort from departments across the university.
